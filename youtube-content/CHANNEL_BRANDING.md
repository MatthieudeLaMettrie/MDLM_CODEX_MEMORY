## 3. Channel description

Two versions. Both are under 1,000 characters, which I believe is YouTube's limit for the channel description. Verify when you paste.

### Version A: beginner-friendly (recommended)

```text
Data & AI in 10: one concept, ten minutes or less.

Short, clear videos on the tools data and AI people actually use: Databricks, Snowflake, BigQuery, Microsoft Fabric, dbt, Power BI, Claude, OpenAI and Gemini.

You'll find:
- Beginner explainers that start from zero
- Fixes for real errors and practical comparisons
- Certification study tips

Every video states who it is for in the first 30 seconds, then gets straight to the point. No filler, no long intros.

New videos regularly. Subscribe if you want to learn data and AI one concept at a time.
```

### Version B: shorter, practitioner tone

```text
Data and AI, explained in 10 minutes or less.

Practical videos on Databricks, Snowflake, BigQuery, Fabric, dbt, Power BI, Claude, OpenAI and Gemini: how things work, how to fix common errors, and how to choose between tools.

For beginners, working data engineers and anyone studying for a certification.
```

Replace "New videos regularly" with a real schedule only when you can keep it (for example "New video every Tuesday").

### Optional line about you (only if it is true)

```text
I'm Matthieu, a data professional who learns by building. I share what I test and what I learn.
```

You can add certifications you hold (your records list SnowPro Core, PL-300, AI-102, DP-600, DP-700 and Databricks Data Engineer Associate). Only list ones you want public and that are current. Do not mention your employer.

## 4. Banner text

The banner is the large image at the top of the channel page. YouTube recommends a 2560 × 1440 px image, with the important text and logo inside a smaller central "safe area" (documented as about 1546 × 423 px) so it isn't cropped on phones and TVs. Verify current guidance in YouTube Studio.

**Main line:** DATA & AI IN 10
**Sub line options:**
1. One concept. Ten minutes. Real demos.
2. Databricks · Snowflake · BigQuery · Fabric · dbt · Power BI · Claude · OpenAI · Gemini
3. Learn data and AI one concept at a time

**Layout suggestion**
- Dark or bright solid background with one accent colour, and large white text.
- Left or centre: "DATA & AI IN 10" in a bold, simple font.
- Below it: the sub line in smaller text, inside the safe area.
- Optional: a small clock or "10:00" icon as a visual motif. Use your own artwork or properly licensed images, and avoid using vendor logos in a way that suggests the vendors endorse the channel.
